The beginning

Of Our Forever

We met on a rain-soaked Tuesday in a bookshop on Bleecker Street, both reaching for the last copy of the same novel. Neither of us bought it.

Seven years of small mornings followed — coffee made two ways, a stubborn plant kept alive, a hundred unremarkable Thursdays that turned out to be the whole point.

Last autumn, on the same street, in the same rain, David asked. Olivia said yes before he finished the sentence.
